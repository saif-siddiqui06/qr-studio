Frequently asked questions

      

        
What is a QR code?
A QR code is a two-dimensional barcode that stores information such as a URL, text, email or Wi-Fi credentials. When scanned, your device shows or acts on that information.

        
How do I create a QR code for my Wi-Fi?
Choose Wi-Fi, enter the network name (SSID), password and encryption type. Anyone who scans the code can join the network without typing the password.

        
Can I make a QR code that opens an email?
Yes. Choose Email and enter the address, subject and message. Scanning it opens the mail app with everything pre-filled.

        
PNG or SVG: which should I download?
Use PNG for websites, chats and documents. Use SVG for print, like posters and business cards, because it scales to any size without blurring.

        
What does error correction do?
Higher error correction lets a code be read even if part of it is damaged or covered, for example by a logo, at the cost of a denser code. Medium suits most uses; use High for print.

        
Is scanning private?
Yes. Scanning runs entirely in your browser. Camera frames and uploaded images never leave your device.

        
My QR code doesn't scan. What should I check?
Make sure the content is complete and correct, keep strong contrast (dark code on a light background), and leave enough size and white space around the code.
